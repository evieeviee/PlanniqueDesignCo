Website pricing in Malaysia can range from a simple DIY setup to a fully custom business platform. The most useful question is not only “How much does a website cost?” but also “What does my business actually need to achieve?”

Quick answer: At Plannique Design Co., a complete one-page Essential website starts at RM499, while a more detailed Signature website with up to seven page views starts at RM1,299. Your final cost may increase if you need extra languages, custom functions, advanced forms, additional content or ongoing services.

What affects the cost of a website?

Two websites can look similar at first glance but require very different amounts of planning, design and development. These are the main factors that normally affect the price:

  • Number of pages or page views: A focused landing page is faster to build than a full multi-page company website.
  • Custom design: A tailored visual direction requires more work than using a basic pre-built template.
  • Content preparation: Pricing may differ depending on whether you provide your own text and images.
  • Languages: Additional languages require duplicated layouts, translated content and extra testing.
  • Functions: Booking forms, product catalogues, payment systems and memberships need more development.
  • SEO and tracking: Search Console, analytics, structured data and technical optimisation may be included or charged separately.
  • Support: Some projects include only launch support, while others include long-term updates and maintenance.

Is the cheapest website always the best choice?

Not always. A low price can be suitable when the project is simple and the scope is clear. Problems usually happen when important items are not discussed before work begins.

Before choosing a designer, ask whether the website will be mobile responsive, who owns the domain and files, how revisions work, whether the contact buttons are tested, what happens after launch and whether there are future charges.

How to choose the right website budget

Choose a one-page website when:

  • You offer a small number of services.
  • Your main goal is WhatsApp enquiries.
  • You are launching a new business and need to start quickly.
  • Your content can be explained clearly on one scrolling page.

Choose a multi-page website when:

  • You need separate pages for services, products or locations.
  • Customers normally research before contacting you.
  • You want a more premium and established brand presentation.
  • You plan to add detailed SEO content over time.

What should you prepare before requesting a quotation?

A clear brief helps your designer recommend the right structure and avoids unnecessary charges later. Prepare:

  • Your business name, logo and contact details.
  • A list of services or products you want to highlight.
  • Examples of website styles you like.
  • Your preferred colours and brand direction.
  • The main action you want visitors to take.
  • Your desired launch date.
  • Any extra requirements such as additional languages, forms or maps.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a small-business website?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, how quickly content is provided and the number of revision rounds. A focused landing page is normally faster than a multi-page website with custom content.

Do I need to buy a domain before starting?

No. You can discuss the design and structure first, then purchase the domain before launch. Make sure the domain is registered under an account you control.

Can I update the website later?

Yes. Simple content changes can be handled after launch, while larger additions such as new pages or custom functions may require a separate quotation.

Does a website guarantee a first-page Google ranking?

No legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ranking. Good technical setup, useful content, strong local signals and ongoing optimisation can improve your chances over time.

Is a one-page website good for SEO?

It can perform well for a focused service and location, but a multi-page site usually provides more space to target different services and search topics individually.
