Fleet management, communication and integrated systems.

MyGPS was founded in 2007 as a complete fleet management solution provider that specializes in Fleet Management / Vehicle Tracking Solution, Digital Two-way Radio Communications and Integrated System.

Backed by skilled engineers, technology partners and R&D specialists, MyGPS provides end-to-end solutions covering field hardware, command centre software, system integration, operation management and system design services.
